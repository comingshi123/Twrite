How do I choose the right place?

Compare your budget, location, facilities, and rental terms. Visit the property before making a decision.

Are prices and availability guaranteed?

No. Confirm current prices, availability, and property details directly with the owner.

What should I check before paying?

Review the rental agreement, included costs, and property conditions, and verify the owner before sending payment.
