A little planning goes a long way

TIPS FOR FINDING
THE RIGHT LODGING

Use this quick checklist to make a confident choice.

1

Set Your Budget

Plan a monthly amount that leaves room for food, transport, and school expenses.

2

Choose a Convenient Location

Look near your school to save time and keep transportation costs down.

3

Check the Facilities

Ask about utilities, internet, safety, and which amenities are included.

4

Visit Before Renting

Inspect the room and review the rental terms before sending any payment.
