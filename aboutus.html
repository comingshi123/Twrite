ABOUT RENTME

RENTME is a student-focused service that makes finding a safe, comfortable place to stay near school less stressful. We bring affordable lodging, boarding houses, and rooms into one easy starting point.

We believe every student deserves a place where they can rest, study, and feel welcome—without losing sight of their budget.

Our Mission

To help students find affordable lodging that fits their needs.

Our Vision

To become a trusted platform for the student housing search.
